# M2 Cross-device Replication

Frozen test: `fixed1` vs `phasegate4to1` vs phase-blind `timegate4to1` at budget **1.25×**. Median paired PhaseGate/Fixed QPS gain was **+80.50%** (10,000-resample paired-run bootstrap interval +80.40% to +80.91%). Median paired PhaseGate/TimeGate gain was **+3.02%** (+1.33% to +3.12%). Median TimeGate high-cap overlap with actual DECODE was **12.30%**. Device results are not pooled with M4 Pro or old M2 Pro runs.
